You use AI every day.
But that may still be consumption.

You write a prompt, you use what comes back. That alone is genuinely useful. But taking the idea in your head, giving it a shape, and putting it in someone else’s hands — that part is still out of reach. Once you move to the making side, your understanding of AI deepens. You start to feel what it can and cannot do, and the thing you imagined becomes something people actually use.

The Manus Shokunin Project is those first two months.

Shokunin (職人): the craftsman’s mindset — the pursuit of depth and mastery in one’s work, beyond mere technical skill.

02

How the two months work

STEP 01FIRST HALF

Start small, and ship it

You take small apps all the way to public release — two is the guide for the first half. What matters is not the count, but running the whole make-and-ship loop early.

STEP 02SECOND HALF

Build your signature piece

You choose the thing you genuinely want to put into the world and start on your signature piece, taking it to release quality with the Fellow’s review each week.

STEP 03FINAL STRETCH

Improve it after launch

Once your signature piece is live, you ship at least one improvement based on what real users tell you. Building it is not where this ends.

The requirement　Release at least one app during the two months. The counts above are a guide, not a quota.

09

Frequently asked questions

Is it really okay to have no coding experience at all?＋

Yes. Manus does the development itself; your role is to decide what to build, direct it, and check the result. The Fellow supports you through the usual sticking points in the weekly 1-on-1.

How much time does it take per week?＋

The only fixed commitment is the weekly 15-minute 1-on-1. Building happens at your own pace, though reaching public release does assume you keep carving out time. We will talk through your available hours in the interview.

What do you look for in selection?＋

In a 15-minute online interview we ask about what you want to build, the time you can secure, and your intent to see it through to release. We weigh follow-through more heavily than existing skill.

How many credits do participants receive?＋

Selected participants receive credits monthly, separate from their own Pro plan. The specifics are shared during selection.

Can I take part if I live outside Tokyo?＋

Yes. The program is primarily online, and all three in-person sessions in Tokyo can be joined remotely.

I am not on Manus Pro yet. Can I still apply?＋

Yes, as long as you plan to subscribe before the kickoff on Aug 29.

What if I want to build an iOS app?＋

You will need to register for the Apple Developer Program (paid). Review can take several days, so we recommend starting when you apply.
